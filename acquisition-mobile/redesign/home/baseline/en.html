Before you request a quote

How much does a website cost?

Essential is €299 including tax for a website of up to five pages. Business is €590 including tax with optimisation for search engines and AI assistants. Premium starts at €990 including tax for CRM, back-office and automation features.

How is my app priced?

As a guide, an Essential app costs €1,990 to €4,990 for core features. Business ranges from €4,990 to €9,990 for more screens, user accounts and optional payments. Above €9,990, Premium covers complex projects with back-office tools, integrations and advanced security. Your quote depends on the scope. Monthly plans are available at €49 or €99 depending on your needs.
